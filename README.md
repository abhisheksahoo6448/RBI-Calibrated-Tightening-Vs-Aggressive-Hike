# RBI-Calibrated-Tightening-Vs-Aggressive-Hike
# Target Variable : Inflation Rate
# Exogenous Variables: Policy Repo Rate | Index of Industrial Production (IIP) | Foreign Exchange Rate (INR/USD)

# My Agenda
To See whether the Monetary Policy Committee would increase or decrease the policy repo rate in the future, And if it is increasing then by how much magnitude and how much time it takes to reach the target inflation rate of 4% (Target inflation rate as per RBI guidelines).

# My Observation
As per my findings I think that RBI's Monetary Policy Committee will raise the policy repo rate by 75 basis points in total. First they will increase repo rate by 50 basis points in the month of December and then they will increase it again by 25 basis points by next year in the month of February, changing their stance from calibrated tightening to a little bit more of hawkish style.

# They will do this because of the following reasons:

# 1. As we know the CPI headline inflation rate is rising continously not to mention in the month of October it was 6.09% which is the highest inflation rate in the past 2 years. Also it is well above the higher tolerance band of 6%(as per the RBI rule).
Talking about CPI Headline Inflation, the prices are increasing persistently too:
Headline Inflation rate in August: 4.83%
Headline Inflation Rate in September: 5.54%
Headline Inflation Rate in October : 6.09%
The Inflation rate is increasing is because of the following reasons:
      a. Geopolitical Conflicts : Because of the US-Iran war, the prices of Indian Crude oil basket has increased significantly which has led to the inflation in           the economy.
      b. Surge in Agricultural Inflation: In august food inflation was 5.97%, in the month of September the food inflation was 7.74% and in the month of October            food inflation was 8.94%.
      c. Constant INR depreciation

# 3. Because of the war between US and Iran, the Federal Reserve decided to raise the policy interest rate rate by 25 basis poinst in the month of September, and therefore it is understandable that RBI will also raise the repo rate majorly because of 2 reasons:
       i.  RBI would want repo rate to be higher than the US interest rate to attract the Foreign Institutional Investors.
       ii. As the INR is constantly on a decline, hiking the repo rate will give a cushion to the depreciation pressure.
# 4. As we saw that the real GDP of the economy grew by 7.8% in Q1 FY27, RBI might think that we have enough room to raise the rates without stunting the growth.
